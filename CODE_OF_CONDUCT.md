# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free, technically rigorous community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or employer.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise, constructive, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete harm or risk.
- Respect privacy, credentials, embargoed vulnerabilities, and organizational boundaries.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project-specific constraints without gatekeeping.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, deliberate misinformation, credential exposure, unsafe recovery guidance, and retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private reporting/contact mechanisms. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest recused.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing this project.

This policy is inspired by Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
